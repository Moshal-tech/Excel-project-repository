# Excel Retail & Sales Data Analysis Project

**Data Technician Bootcamp | Week 1 – Microsoft Excel**

## Project Overview

This project was completed as part of my Data Technician Bootcamp, focusing on using Microsoft Excel to analyse, manipulate and visualise retail and sales datasets.

The objective was to develop practical data analysis skills by applying Excel formulas, filtering, sorting, PivotTables, conditional formatting and charts to transform raw data into meaningful business insights.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel** – Data analysis and visualisation
- **Excel Formulas** – SUM, SUMIF, AVERAGE, AVERAGEIF, DATE, MONTH, YEAR, UNIQUE, VLOOKUP and SWITCH
- **PivotTables & PivotCharts** – Data summarisation and reporting
- **Filtering & Sorting** – Data exploration and organisation
- **Conditional Formatting** – Highlighting trends and performance

## Key Skills Demonstrated

### 1. Data Preparation & Manipulation
- Organised and structured retail transaction and sales datasets.
- Applied filtering and sorting to analyse customer demographics, product categories and transaction records.
- Used `DATE`, `MONTH` and `YEAR` to work with transaction dates and support time-based analysis.
- Used `UNIQUE` to identify distinct values within datasets.

### 2. Excel Formulas & Calculations
- Applied `SUM` and `SUMIF` to calculate total sales, commissions and conditional totals.
- Used `AVERAGE` and `AVERAGEIF` to analyse average values across different criteria.
- Practised `VLOOKUP` to retrieve and match information across tables.
- Used `SWITCH` to classify sales performance into **High, Medium and Low** categories based on sales volume.

### 3. PivotTables & Sales Analysis
- Created PivotTables to summarise sales performance by product, county, country and customer age group.
- Analysed bike sales data across different geographical markets.
- Compared product sales volumes to identify sales patterns and differences in performance.

### 4. Data Visualisation
- Created Excel charts to present sales trends and comparisons.
- Used PivotCharts to communicate geographical and demographic sales patterns.
- Applied conditional formatting, colour scales and data bars to highlight performance differences.

## Key Project Outcomes

- Developed practical skills in transforming raw retail data into structured analytical reports.
- Produced PivotTable summaries comparing sales volumes across different product categories and counties.
- Analysed geographical sales performance and customer age groups using bike sales data.
- Automated sales classifications using the `SWITCH` function.
- Improved the ability to communicate data findings through Excel charts and visual reporting.

This project strengthened my foundation in Excel-based data analysis, demonstrating my ability to organise datasets, apply analytical formulas, summarise business information and present findings visually.
